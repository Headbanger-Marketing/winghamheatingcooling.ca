FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Wingham homeowners ask us most.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service in Wingham?

Yes. We offer emergency heating and cooling service across Wingham and the surrounding north Huron area, including rural properties between here, Blyth, and Lucknow.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are a fully licensed and insured HVAC company serving Wingham and Huron County homeowners, with certified technicians who work on all major brands.

My home runs propane or oil heat. Can you service it?

Absolutely. Most rural north Huron properties run propane or oil furnaces, and our technicians service those systems regularly, along with the boilers found in many century farmhouses.

Do you handle oil-to-heat-pump conversions?

Yes. We install cold-climate heat pumps that replace old oil furnaces, handle the removal and rebate paperwork, and can pair the system with Generac backup power for outages.

How much does a service call cost?

We offer free, no-obligation quotes. Diagnosing the issue is straightforward, and we explain all pricing clearly before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Do you work on all brands of furnace and AC?

Yes. Our certified technicians service, repair, and maintain all major furnace and air conditioner brands common to Wingham and the surrounding Huron County area.

Do you offer financing on new equipment?

Yes, we offer financing options on installations and major equipment like heat pumps and mini-splits. Ask us about current plans when you request your free quote.

Do you serve areas outside of Wingham?

Yes. We serve Wingham and nearby north Huron communities including Blyth, Belgrave, Brussels, and Lucknow, plus the rural farm properties in between.

Do you serve areas outside of Wingham, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Wingham and nearby communities including Blyth, Belgrave, Brussels, Lucknow, and the surrounding area.
